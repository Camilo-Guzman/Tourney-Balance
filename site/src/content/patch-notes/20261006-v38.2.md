# More nerfs v38.2 (Open Beta)
## Foot Knight
**Valiant Charge (Ultimate)**
* Lowered charge interruption blast radius to 4 (from 5)

**Have at Thee**

*Uptime buffs not needed, since ultimate is back on 30 seconds cooldown.*
* Reverted to vanilla.

**Inspiring Blow**

*Uptime buffs not needed, since ultimate is back on 30 seconds cooldown.*
* Reverted to vanilla.

**Bull of Ostland**

*Uptime buffs not needed, since ultimate is back on 30 seconds cooldown.*
* Reverted to vanilla.

## Grail Knight
**Virtue of Stoicism**
* Reverted to vanilla.

**Virtue of Discipline**
* Reverted to vanilla.

**Virtue of the Joust**
* Removed weapon movement slowdown removal.
* Increased push and block angle to 70% (from 30%).
* Increased stamina regeneration to 70% (from 30%).

## Shade
**Hungry Wind**

*A less overcooked version of shade. Guaranteed critical strikes increased dps potential too much.*
* Removed guaranteed critical strikes for the duration.
* Each kill during the 10 seconds after leaving Infiltrate extends the movement speed, Power and pass-through by 1 second, up to 10 additional seconds.

## Zealot
**Overhealth**

*While the nerfed version wasn't too strong, overhealth does fit the original selfish design of Zealot from official.*
* Removed

**Unbending Purpose**

*Hookrat 1shs breakpoint only requires 1 skaven property.*
* Increased power to 10% (from 5%)
* Decreased melee power to 10% (from 15%)

**Crusade**
* Additionally grants a chance to avoid damage equal to the movement speed bonus.
* Including Crusade (5% per Fiery Faith stack), Devotion (30% movement speed after taking damage), movement speed trinket (5%) and speed potions (50%) also add to this chance, up to 100%.

**Devotion**

*No slowdown on weapons is too strong*
* Changed to only immune to slowing debuffs (bile, plague, fire, etc.) now.
* Knockback immunity to ranged projectiles and Warpfire unchanged.

**Redemption through Blood**
* Reverted to vanilla.

**Calloused Without and Within**
* Reverted to vanilla.

## Warrior Priest
**Unyielding Blessing**

*The problematic interaction with Unchained no longer exists.*
* Reverted duration to 10 seconds (from 7 seconds)

## Unchained
**Blood Magic & Abandon**
* Health to Ult conversion only works, if actual health was lost.

**Fuel to the Fire**

*More official realm like version of this talent.*
* Removed WHC shout stagger
* No longer clears overcharge
* Instead Blood Magic does not generate overcharge for 15 seconds

**Wildfire**

*More official realm like version of this talent.*
* Removed WHC shout stagger

## Weapons
**Halberd**

*Initial compensation for Mercenary losing Assassin. Overtuned state.*
* Reverted light 1 to heavy 2 chain speed buff to 0% (from 23%)
* Increased heavy 1 headshot and critical strike bonus damage by 40% (boss headshots unaffected)
* Increased heavy 2 headshot and critical strike bonus damage by 75% (boss headshots unaffected)
* Increased light attack down and push attack armor damage and crit damage by 64%

**Bretonnian Sword and Shield**

*Buffs for an unused weapon.*
* Increased infantry damage on light 1 by 100%
* Added shield break to light 2 and light 3 (shield bash)
* Reduced chain timing for light 2 into light 3 to 0.6 (from 0.75)
* Reduced chain timing for light 3 into light 1 to 0.32 (from 0.65)

## Stagger Talents
**Bulwark**
* Changed debuff to real 10% instead of stacking it with the stagger damage bonus multiplier

**Mainstay**
* Mainstay marks only apply to elites
