**Bull of Ostland**

## Zealot
**Overhealth**
* Removed

**Unbending Purpose**

*Hookrat 1shs breakpoint only requires 1 skaven property.*
* Increased power to 10% (from 5%)
* Decreased melee power to 10% (from 15%)

**Crusade**
* Additionally grants a chance to avoid damage equal to the movement speed bonus.
* Including Crusade (5% per Fiery Faith stack), Devotion (30% movement speed after taking damage), movement speed trinket (5%) and speed potions (50%) also add to this chance, up to 100%.

**Devotion**

*No slowdown on ranged weapons felt to weird*
* Readded slowdown for ranged weapons.

**Redemption through Blood**
* Changed 5% melee damage per missing half stamina shield to 5% melee damage per half stamina shield restored, lasting until stamina is next restored by this talent.
* Additionally reduces Heart of Iron's cooldown by 1 second for every half stamina shield restored.

**Calloused Without and Within**

*A minor adjustment, since damage reduction has already been universally a very strong pick.*
* Additionally Heart of Iron's cooldown is now a fixed 100 seconds (from 90).
