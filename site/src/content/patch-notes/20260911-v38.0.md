# Yet Another Gigantic Patch (OPEN BETA/WIP)
*This time we got a preferrential treatment for the elf patch, with all of her 4 careers receiving significant buffs to side-grade talents. Initially I set out to fixing and tweaking previous beta changes and adding a few new changes from the board. Here we are with another massive changelog that should allow more build and team variety, while also introducing more stimulating gameplay, if you wish to engage with it.* - chen

# Career Changes
*Why so many buffs? That's what happens when a longstanding meta has mostly settled, where alternative options turn out to be either too niche or simply not effective enough. The goal wasn't to bring everything to be on par, but instead nerf obvious outliers and create some diversity. To stop the vagueposting start reading the FK changes.*

## Foot Knight
* **Hero Time and Baseline ULT**: *Hero Time is overpowered. Being able to spam 70% cooldown reduction on demand is simply too strong. The 30 second internal cooldown brings it more in line and allows us to have Hero Time give back your entire ult like on official. This refund will only trigger when your ult is not ready, so that the internal cooldown isn't  just going off whenever. Since Hero Time got significantly nerfed, the baseline ult could also be reverted back to its original 30 seconds. More power back to the baseline.*
* **Have at Thee! and Inspiring Blow**: *Foot Knight revolves around staggering enemies, so having talents grant buffs on staggering elites is cool, until you play on C3 with Deathwish. With the addition of the new Mainstay, we extended the condition to grant buffs when applying stagger counts via Mainstay. Given improved consistency, Have at Thee and Inspiring Blow had their effects reverted back to their official values.*
* **Rock of Reikland**: *This has seen little use, due to it being less effective than an item property. Changing it to 30% BCR and 30% Stamina Regeneration opens up item properties for more build variety. Meanwhile being a team-wide passive effectively grants the team more value, if the Foot Knight happens do be out of range or dead.*
* ***Numb to Pain**: *3 seconds is too short to make something happen with invulnerability. 5 seconds creates a longer window for using items, helping team mates or trying to get some temp hp back.*

* **Ultimate**
    * CD reverted to 30s.
* **Hero Time**
    * Reverted to 100% CD refund.
    * Added 30s internal cooldown.
* **Have at Thee!**
    * Procs on applying mainstay stagger count.
    * Reverted buff duration to 10s (from 15s).
* **Inspiring Blow**
    * Procs on applying mainstay stagger count.
    * Reverted buff effect to 100% cooldown regeneration for 0.5s (from 200% for 1.5s).
* **Rock of Reikland**
    * Global passive for team.
    * Added 30% bcr.
    * Added 30% stam recovery.
* **Numb to Pain**
    * Invulnerability increased to 5s (from 3s).

* **No Dawdling**

    * Reverted bomb dupe buff to stay after smokescreen ends.

* **Exhilarating Vapours**
    * Fixed a bug/tech that grants extra THP by stepping in and out of the smoke cloud, resetting icd timers.

## Witch Hunter Captain
*After trying out different iterations, this version of I Shall Judge You All is more in line with what the vanilla talent tries to do. The vanilla talent essentially buffs the horde damage through this talent. In this iteration, we simply have this aura applying Witch-Hunt to surrounding enemies last longer. For now the Special Tag is staying in as an extra utility, because the increased crit and stagger up-time from The Unending Hunt is difficult to compete with.*
* **I Shall Judge You All**
    * Apply Witch Hunt to all enemies within Animosity's range and all specials for the duration of Animosity.
    * Headshotting Witch-Hunted enemies extends the duration by 1s.

## Bounty Hunter
*These changes started of with adding 10% attack speed for the team from Rile the Mob. Since Bounty Hunter has always been in a flex role, contributing both in melee and ranged, we decided to expand his kit to support his playstyle better. Moving melee kills reloading your weapon from salvaged ammunition to the passive, opens up more build options and improves his overall gameplay flow.*

* Blessed Kill (Passive)
    * Added Melee Kills reload weapon from Salvaged Ammunition
* Steel Crescendo
    * Corrected talent description to melee power (from power)
* Weight of Fire
    * Increased power per ammo in clip  to 3% (from 1%)
        * *This buff was necessary, because at 2% Open Wounds was more effective while increasing team damage as well.*
* Cruel Fortune
    * Grants an additional critical strike (6s Cooldown).
        * *This buff opens up another layer of skill expression, for those willing to juggle 2 crit cooldowns. This Cruel Fortune stack grants you a guaranteed critical strike (both melee and ranged), but it can get consumed at the same time as Blessed Shots. By using a melee attack you can separate those cooldowns, giving you effectively at crit every 3 seconds. This enables pure ranged gameplay on Bounty Hunter, complex kill combos or Swift Slaying uptime, depending on how you use it.*
* Rile the Mob
    * Added 10% attack speed for the team
        * *While adding more team buffs is dangerous for powercreep, BH takes a unique spot. If you fully commit on teamwide attack speed buffs Merc, RV, BH means that you don't have access to the prime hard frontliners anymore. I won't spoil more about team builds, have fun exploring them.*
* Salvaged Ammunition
    * Increased ammo refunded to 25% (from 20%)
        * *This tweak decreases the out of ammo downtime. We decided leave in the out of ammo condition as that is what keeps the talent exciting, when going high risk, high reward with a Hunter crossbow.*
    * Makes Blessed Kills restore 20% of clip size.



## Waystalker
*After some feedback on the previous patch it seems that Waystalkers ammo sustain for sustained damage and sniping with Longbow became too difficult to keep up on Piercing Shot or Loaded Bow. The baseline ammo regen is now buffed and always available, but only kicks in below 50% ammo. This makes the level 20 row simply be a chocie between improved self-heal, ult-regen, or group-heal. Buffing Spirit Arrows to 10% is an interesting spot where the 10% CDR trinket provides more value than the 20% CDR from Asrai Focus due to tick timing.*

*Ricochet in the previous iteration didn't open up more build options and only reinforced the hagbane spam. This time Ricochet simply grants your projectile the ability to seek out enemies after a bounce. To allow consecutive and natural usage, but limit spam, it requires extra charging time as well as 10% ult cooldown. It's notable that the 10% are drained over 10s and disable you ultimate for that duration.*

* Ultimate
    * Conservative shooter can proc. Limited to 1 ammo returned per arrow.
* Amaranthe
    * Increased ammo regen to 3 from 1.
    * Only regens ammo while below 50% ammo
* Drakira's Alacrity
    * Reverted attack speed bonus to 15% (from 20%)
* Isha's Embrace
    * Allow passive ammo regen as well
* Spirit Arrows
    * Removed additional 1 ammo regen
    * Increased cooldown reduction to 10% (from 5%)
* Ricochet 
    * Fully charging for 1 second grants ricochet projectiles true-flight.
    * Applying true-flight costs 10% ult cooldown drained over 10 seconds.
        * This cooldown drain debuff can stack and disables ult.
        * Trueflight application requires ult bar to be at least 10%.
        * Fixed ricocheting after enemy cleave.
        * Won't pierce enemies and randomly pull patrols.

## Handmaiden
*Handmaiden has been on my radar for a while, since she is one of the most bland careers in the game. While she does offer mechanical intense gameplay, most of her kit are just stat buffs. This set of changes raises the mechanical skill ceiling and her ability to frontline and just add a new layer of mechanics more experienced players can play around with. But first off, I reverted Sting to Execute for no reason other than she does not have Sting on vanilla and Sting is far too competitive on a career that has access to high crit rate, high attack speed with the best weapons for headshotting.*

* **Mechanics Changes**:
- *Jump-cancelling and Bhops were added to Handmaidens ult to make it more fun to use.*
- *Dance of Seasons also allows Handmaiden to start blocking instantly. On host/low-ping you can now play hyper-aggressive as any attack can be blocked in time, if you see it. As for high-ping players you can now start blocking while the attack animation is still finishing. Apart from raising the skill ceiling for aggressive play, this was another reason, why Sting got replaced and Bendy got removed.*
- *Oak Guard now allows you to briefly taunt non boss enemies by pushing them. With instant block this should enable something similar to hard frontlining on Handmaiden*
- *Dance of Blades is often used as the ranged breakpoint talent. The instant dodges are added to allow a more mechanical intense gameplay. You can use it to cancel mid-dodge to correct a dodge into an overhead or you can chain dodges to create more space when sniping.*
- *Lastly since we removed Sting, the healing received from Bendy was moved to Heart of Oak, while Birch Stance received a buff to make the talent a bit more interesting for specific situations where damage is still taken while holding block. Quiver of Plenty got a buff, cause why not. Javelins only get 1 extra ammo, but Longbow becomes a strong contender now besides Moonfire and Javs.*

* THP
    * Reverted Sting to Execute
* Ultimate
    * Added jump-cancel and bhops.
* Dance of Seasons (Passive)
    * Added blocking starts immediately.
* Bendy (Passive)
    * Removed 40% increased healing.
* Oak Guard (Passive)
    * Add perk to be listed
    * Additional effect: Pushing enemies (excluding Lords and Bosses) taunts them for 2 seconds.
* Dance of Blades
    * Added dodging starts immediately (1s cooldown each).
    * Increased buff duration to 6 seconds.
* Heart of Oak
    * Added 40% increased healing.
* Birch Stance
    * Added 30% damage reduction while blocking.
* Quiver of Plenty
    * Increased ammo to 100% (from 70%)

## Shade
*Shade has always been a self-sufficient hypercarry DPS leaning into a glass-cannon playstyle. The rework aims to enable such a playstyle, but this is likely the first of many iterations. Exploit Weakness received a massive buff increasing her damage ceiling significantly under the right conditions. Meanwhile movement speed on her level 25 row was moved to the passive. This leaves 2 open talent slots besides Blood Drinker. Elthrai's Mockery let's Shade keep bosses easier on her and is the simple frontline DPS choice providing a flat damage buff. But it also makes obtaining Blur easier and prevents enemies aggroing onto teammater after a blur. Lingering Shadows is the prime choice for extended Blur gameplay. As for Hungry Wind it makes all your attacks to be considered as Backstabs.*

* Passive (Assassin's Blade)
    * (Added Gladerunner): 10% movement speed
    * (Added Spring-Heeled Assassin): 10% movement speed on crit or entering stealth.
* Exploit Weakness
    * Poison, Bleed, and Burn each individually increase damage dealt by 20%.
    * Stacks additive, up to 60% against a target suffering from all three.
* Blood Drinker
    * Additionally entering stealth reduces damage taken by 20.0% for 5 seconds.
* Elthrai’s Mockery (NEW) (replaces Spring-Heeled Assassin)
    * Hitting enemies taunts them for 5s.
    * Deal 25% more damage to enemies Kerillian has taunted.
* Lingering Shadow (NEW) (replaces Gladerunner)
    * Duration of Blur increased by 0.5s (to a total of 2s).
    * Attacking an enemy no longer removes Blur invisibility.
* Hungry Wind
    * All your attacks are considered backstabs.
    * Reduced duration to 6 seconds

# Stagger Talent Changes
*Mainstay needed a nerf to make it not too easy to apply. At the same time we are reverting most Stagger talents to allow different team comps to have access to better melee boss damage. Specifically Mainstay is kept out of the plus comp and its sister variant, as it already has access to plenty of buffs/debuffs from WHC and SoTT.*

*Mercenary with halberd is very strong and this lowers the damage potential of halberd requiring consecutive hits. On Slayer coghammer and pickaxe strongly synergise with Assassin. Replacing it with Mainstay empowers great axe/hammer. Coghammer will be be buffed in retrospect, if needed. Zealot with Mainstay should have similar damage potential, but it is slightly delayed if you were able to have 100% headshot rate before, but now you are helping your team's melee damage. Similarly Pyromancer prefers Enhanced Power, while Mainstay serves as the melee support option.*
