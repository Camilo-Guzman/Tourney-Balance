# Talent Balance v38
We’ve put this patch together with a ton of helpful suggestions from the community, and we want to say a huge thank you for all your input! Our goal with these changes is to open up more talent choices within careers, spice things up with new playstyles, and make room for completely new team comps.

While we’ve gone through countless discussions and iterations with many of you, we know the final result might not be perfect. Nothing is set in stone - anything can be reverted if it stops fitting the vision for Tourney Balance.

Here is a quick breakdown of our guiding philosophy for this patch:
1. Balanced for Dutch Spice: We balance around the highest tournament difficulty because we want to see true build variety at this level of play.
2. Official Builds Still Work: If a new player jumps into TB with a build from the official game, it will still work and function exactly as they expect.
3. Additive Balance & Targeted Reworks: Full reworks are strictly reserved for extreme outliers. Otherwise, our approach is additive—we prefer to build on top of existing talents and careers to unlock new possibilities, ensuring that classic builds remain completely intact.

<summary>Detailed explanation</summary>

* Heroism: Release asap deals 130% damage. Release at latest deals 400% damage. It's slightly more than 390% (3*130%) to compensate for inaccuracies from charge time. The bonus scales from 0%-270% linearly based on additional charge time 0s-1.35s (counted from the earliest point a charge attack can be released). This does not affect GK ult.
* Charging a heavy attack for up to further 1.35 seconds increases bonus damage linearly up to 270% damage on top of base 30% damage.
* Targeted buff for bretonnian longsword and bret sword & shield measured based on charge time of exe sword.
* 20 frames windup, 20 frames max charge time, e.g. charge 1s (being earliest release window for heavy attack) gives 10 damage, then charge another 1s, gives 20 damage. 1.35 seconds value for linear damage scaling based on charge time.\
* Most weapons auto release at full charge, only bret sword and bret sword and shield can full charge for max damage. Higher charge higher risk of getting hit, but better opportunity cost for windup time vs release time, i.e. charging 1 long heavy deals damage of 2 heavies; saving the time of having to release 2 heavy attacks, but risking hits during a longer charged attack.
