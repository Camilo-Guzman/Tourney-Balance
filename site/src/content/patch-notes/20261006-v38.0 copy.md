# Balance Adjustments v38.1
The v38 patch was fairly big and we were also aware before release that some of the changes were fairly overtuned. Hope you had fun with those changes, but it is now time to go back to a more tame version of Tourney Balance.

## Foot Knight
**Inspiring Blow**
*Mainstay with Bull of Ostland was providing slightly too much value.*
* Reverted cooldown on real stagger to vanilla 200% (from 100%) for 0.5s.
* Lowered cooldown on Mainstay stagger to 20% (from 50%) for 0.5s.

## Handmaiden
**Renewal**
*The effect is purely dependent on player skill.*
* Only shares overflowing temporary health gained through level 5 talents.

## Shade
**Grim Fortune**
*Bloodfetcher with this had too much sustain.*
* Removed ranged crit

**Exploit Weakness**
*This talent was too strong, since the guaranteed bleed gave access to a 20% debuff."
* Damage increase per DoT type on enemy reduced to 10% (from 20%)

**Khaine's Counter**
*Small tweak on Shade's dps potential.*
* Auto-backstab for up to 2 targets.

**Ruthless Precision**
*Small tweak on Shade's dps potential.*
* Auto-backstab for up to 2 targets.

## Zealot
*100 overhealth was obviously too strong as it essentially extended everyones health fairly consistent by 100. We are putting this on 20 overhealth, but separate per player.*
**Fiery Faith: Overhealth**
* Changed to 20 overhealth per player.

## Unchained
*Overcharging is a penalty mechanic and can be prevented. In comparison the ultimate Living Bomb should not deal ff, because it we want to encourage actively using it to contribute positively to the combat.*
**Overcharge explosion**
* Readded friendly fire.

**Bomb Balm**
*Having to choose between stagger or temporary health should make the talents feel more even.*
* Removed AoE stagger from Bomb Balm

**Enfeebling Flames**
*This has become more accessible and there are lots of other damage reduction sources, thus it should have lower value.*
* Lowered enemy damage dealt reduction to 10% (from 20%)
