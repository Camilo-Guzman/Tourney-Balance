# v38.3

## Foot Knight
**Protective Presence**
* Reduced range to 10 (from 20)

**That's Bloody Teamwork**
* Reduced range to 10 (from 20)

**Comrades in Arms**
* Reduced range to 10 (from 20)
* Added range limit to guard specific player mode.
* Adjusted swapping guarded to trigger on pressing "Weapon Inspect"
* Detailed behaviour:
    * Closest ally mode works the same.
    * Guard ally mode shows an additional comrades in arms icon. The number is the guarded players id and the green outline means the player is within range.
    * If guarded player leaves the range, the icon gets a red outline, and buff is given to closest ally.
    * If guarded reenters the range, the icon becomes green again, and buff is given to guarded ally.

## Witch Hunter Captain
*In the previous patches we tried to buff ISJYA and Fervency to the power level of The Unending Hunt, which inflated values and also wasn't making those talents more competitive in the meta. This time we lower the crits of The Unending Hunt to provide roughly the same amount of crits as a base ult. This gives room for ISJYA and Fervency to shine, without having to inflate those talents with new mechanics.*

**I Shall Judge You All**
* Removed revealing specials within range of Animosity

**Fervency**
* Lowered duration to 8 seconds (from 10 seconds)
* Removed guaranteed crit stacks

**The Unending Hunt**
* Lowered crit chance bonus for team mates to 10% (from 15%)
    * 8.3% less total crit provided compared to base ult
        * `90/(90*0.6) * (100% - 3*15%) = 0.917`
    * 1% less total crit provided compared to base ult (using 10% cdr trinket)
        * `(90*0.9)/(90*0.5) * (100% - 3*15%) = 0.99`

## Bounty Hunter
**Indiscriminate Blast**
*This talent is all about damage, but has always been feast or famine depending on whether the incoming wave was armored or not. This is a more experimental change to pivot away from pure damage inflation. Instead the we added some crowd control against armored enemies to Bounty Hunters ultimate and reverted the cooldown reduction.*

* Reverted cooldown reduction to 25% (from 60%)
* Pellets have massively increased cleave and stagger.

## Zealot
**Crusade**
*This was an experimental change. Given the buffed Smite and the Chasten passive, the talent has enough potential on the Volley Crossbow build.*

* Removed chance to avoid damage

## Unchained
*Since Unchained is powerful enough already we would like to avoid buffing her level 30 talents. These adjustments bring the talents closer to the power level of Wildfire. Additionally it weakens the synergy of Bomb Balm with the heal bubble from Warrior Priest. Also Numb to Pain is getting renamed to Numb to Flames.*

**Numb to Pain**
* Renamed to *Numb to Flames* to be discernable from Numb to Pain from Foot Knight.

**Fuel for the Fire**
* Lowered power per stack to 3% (from 5%)

**Bomb Balm**
* Lowered temp health granted to 15 (from 30)

## Bretonnian Sword and Shield
*We were willing to make some more drastic changes to this weapon since not very few, if any, have played it extensively. Light 1 is a difficult-to-headshot-with attack that could do with more damage. Shield breaking was added to Light 1 and 2 as this weapon is incapable of dealing with shields due to its moveset and attack speed. We also reduced chain timers on these attacks as the delays, thus overall time to go from landing Light 2 to landing Light 3, did not feel justified. We could have adjusted damage but the slowness of the weapon would remain and the latter felt a more viable approach. Heavy 1 and 3 were made safer to facilitate a synergy with GK's Heroism talent.*
### Light 1
* Non-Crit Infantry damage increased by 100% (from 1 to 2)
### Light 2
* Added Shield Breaking
* Reduced the time to chain into Light 3 by 0.15 seconds (from 0.65 to 0.5 seconds)
### Light 3
* Added Shield Breaking
* Reduced the time to chain into Light 1 by 0.33 seconds (from 0.65 to 0.32 seconds)
### Heavy 1
* Added blocking whilst charging the attack.
### Heavy 3
* Added blocking whilst charging the attack.
